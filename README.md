# 12 Reader

12 Reader reads articles aloud on your Mac. All speech is made on your Mac. Nothing is sent to a server.

This repository has the downloads and the updates for the app. It does not have the source code.

## Download

1. Open the [latest release](https://github.com/shtb-dev/12-reader/releases/latest).
2. Download the `.dmg` file.
3. Open the `.dmg` file and drag 12 Reader into the Applications folder.

The app runs on macOS 12 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel Macs.

## Updates

The app finds and downloads new versions from this repository. When an update is ready, the app asks you to restart. You can also select **12 Reader > Check for Updates…**.

## Problems

To tell us about a problem, [open an issue](https://github.com/shtb-dev/12-reader/issues/new). In the app, select **Help > Export Diagnostics…** and attach the file.
